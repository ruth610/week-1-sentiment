## Key Findings

✅ **Sentiment predicts next-day returns** with statistical significance  
✅ **Positive sentiment** correlates with positive returns  
✅ **Negative sentiment** predicts increased volatility  
✅ **Combined signals** (sentiment + technical indicators) outperform single indicators  
✅ **Technology stocks** show stronger sentiment-return correlations
